1. Who we are

Jallow Transporters Ltd ("JT", "we", "us") is a UK company. We run this website, the booking and tracking pages and the JT Moroccan Gems shop. We decide how your personal details are used, so under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) we are the "controller" of them.

2. What we collect

When you book a shipment

  • Your name, phone / WhatsApp number, town or city, and if you give them, your email, address, postcode and customer number.
  • The receiver's (or sender's) name, phone number and town.
  • What you are sending: the items, number of items, weight, preferred date, any notes, and any photos you upload of your goods.

When you order from the shop

  • Your name, phone / WhatsApp number, town or city, and if you give them, your email, address and postcode.
  • What you ordered, how you want to receive it, and any notes.

When you track a booking or order

  • The reference, invoice or customer number you type, and the phone number if you add it to see the amount owed.

When we deal with you

  • Your bookings, invoices and payments, and messages you send us on WhatsApp, by phone or email.

Technical details

  • Like every website, our servers see your device's internet (IP) address when you use the site. We use it to stop misuse, for example too many tracking searches in a short time.
  • We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use tracking or analytics tools that follow you around the web.

If you give us someone else's details (for example the person receiving your parcel), please let them know, so they know why we may contact them.

3. Why we use it

  • To carry out your booking or order — to confirm it, collect or receive your goods, ship them, keep you updated, hand them to the receiver, invoice you and take payment. (Legal basis: it is needed for our contract with you.)
  • To keep our records and accounts — invoices and payment records we must keep by law. (Legal basis: legal obligation.)
  • To run our business safely — to recognise returning customers, deal with questions and claims, and stop fraud or misuse of the website. (Legal basis: our legitimate interests.)

We contact you about your bookings and orders. We do not sell your details, and we do not send you marketing unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

Only with people who need it to do the job:

  • Our team in the UK and at the destination, who handle your booking.
  • Carriers and agents — airlines, shipping lines, customs and clearing agents, and the people who hand over your goods at the other end. They get what they need to move and deliver your shipment, such as the receiver's name and phone number.
  • Companies that run our systems — our website host and our secure database provider, who store the details for us and may only use them on our instructions. Our pages also load fonts from Google, which means Google sees your device's IP address.
  • WhatsApp — if you choose to message us there, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that chat.
  • Authorities — customs, HMRC, the police or a court, where the law requires it.

5. Details sent abroad

Your shipment goes to another country, so the details needed to deliver it go with it — to our team and agents there (for example in The Gambia). This is needed to carry out your booking. Some of the companies that run our systems may also store details outside the UK; where they do, we use providers that protect them with the safeguards UK law requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • Bookings, invoices and payments: 6 years after the end of the tax year they belong to, because UK tax law requires it.
  • Photos you upload of your goods: deleted after 1 year.
  • Enquiries that never became a booking or order: only as long as we need them, then deleted.

7. What is saved on your phone or computer

To save you typing, our pages store a few things in your own browser or in our app, not on our servers: "My shipments" (the booking and order references you made or saved on this phone or computer, with the phone number they were booked with, so you can see where they are in one tap), your shop basket, and the contact details you last used in the shop. You can remove a shipment from "My shipments" with its × button, or remove everything by clearing this site's data in your browser settings (in the app: by uninstalling it).

If you turn on notifications in our app, we keep your phone's notification address (a code from Google or Apple, not your phone number) and the references in your "My shipments" list on our server, so we can tell you when a booking is confirmed or a shipment leaves or arrives. The notification only says that step, never your name or the price. Turn notifications off in your phone's settings, or remove a shipment from the list, and we stop.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the details we hold about you;
  • correct details that are wrong;
  • delete your details (we may still have to keep invoice and payment records for the time the law requires);
  • stop or limit how we use them, or object to how we use them;
  • send your details to you or someone else in a common format.

Just message or email us using the details below. It is free, and we will reply within one month.

9. Contact us or complain

Questions about your details, or want to use one of your rights? Email jallowtransportersltd@outlook.com or WhatsApp / call +44 7831 005961.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
